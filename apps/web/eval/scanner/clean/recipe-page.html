Thai Green Curry

Fragrant, creamy and ready in 35 minutes — my weeknight favourite.

⏱ Prep 15 min 🔥 Cook 20 min 🌶 Medium heat

Ingredients

Servings 4

    Method

    Tap a step to mark it done.

    1. Heat the coconut oil in a wok or large pan over medium-high heat. Add the green curry paste and fry for 1–2 minutes until fragrant.
    2. Pour in about a third of the coconut milk and stir until the paste dissolves and the oil starts to separate.
    3. Add the sliced chicken thighs and cook for 4–5 minutes, turning, until no longer pink on the outside.
    4. Add the remaining coconut milk, the stock, makrut lime leaves and the palm sugar. Bring to a gentle simmer.
    5. Add the Thai aubergines and green beans. Simmer for 8–10 minutes until the vegetables are just tender.
    6. Season with fish sauce and lime juice. Taste — it should be salty, slightly sweet and gently spicy.
    7. Turn off the heat and stir through the Thai basil leaves and sliced red chilli.
    8. Serve with steamed jasmine rice.

    Tip: Don't shake the coconut milk tin. Scoop the thick cream off the top for frying the paste — it gives a much richer flavour.
