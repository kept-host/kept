Our story

We met in the queue for the last train out of Oxford in 2019 — Daniel had the wrong ticket, Maya had a spare. Four years, two cities, one very opinionated cat (hello, Biscuit) and a soggy proposal on a Welsh hilltop later, we're getting married.

We'd love nothing more than to celebrate with the people who got us here. Please come hungry and bring dancing shoes.
