Frequently asked questions

Do I need a cookie banner?

No. Driftwood doesn't set cookies or store personal data, so most teams in the EU don't need consent for it. Check with your own counsel if you're unsure.

What happens if I go over my event limit?

We keep collecting. You'll get an email and can upgrade within 30 days — we never drop your data.

Can I import data from Google Analytics?

Yes, we import up to 24 months of GA4 history on Startup and Scale plans.

Is there a free plan?

Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial. Open-source projects and non-profits can apply for a free Startup plan.
