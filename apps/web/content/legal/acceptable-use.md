# Acceptable use

Last updated: 16 October 2026

kept hosts web pages that its users make, by hand or with an AI agent. Pages go live the moment they are published. We don't check them before they go live, and we don't adopt what they say: each page is the responsibility of the person who published it.

These rules apply to everyone who publishes on kept, signed in or not, and to everything published through kept's API or by an agent on your behalf. If your agent publishes it, you published it.

## The rules

You may not use kept for any of the following. Each rule has a number, and when we act on a page we tell you which rule it broke.

### Rule 1: Stealing logins or payment details, or pretending to be someone you are not

Don't collect passwords, one-time codes, card or bank details, wallet recovery phrases or private keys from people under false pretences. Don't present a page as a company, bank, government body, platform or person you are not. A mockup or clone of a real product is fine when it collects nothing, sends nothing anywhere and is clearly your own project.

### Rule 2: Fraud

Don't use kept to cheat people out of money or personal details: no fake giveaways, investments, shops, jobs, prizes, refunds or tech support.

### Rule 3: Malware

Don't distribute malware, and don't trick people into downloading files, apps or browser extensions, for example by presenting them as an update, an invoice, a viewer or a fix.

### Rule 4: Child sexual abuse material

Never publish child sexual abuse material. A page reported as such is hidden at once, before anyone reviews it. When we confirm it, we remove it, nobody can download it, and we report it to the authorities as the law requires.

### Rule 5: Other illegal content

Don't publish anything else that is illegal, including content that infringes someone else's rights, such as copyright, trademarks or personal rights.

### Rule 6: Harassment, hate and violence

Don't harass or threaten people, and don't incite hatred or violence against anyone.

### Rule 7: Spam and overloading kept

Don't use kept to send or support spam, and don't try to overload kept or get around its limits.

## How we check pages

- **An automated check.** When a page is published, replaced or restored to an earlier version, an automated check reads an extract of it and looks for the kinds of pages that rules 1 to 4 describe. We also run it again when a page is reported, or when its traffic suddenly jumps. How the check works, and what it sends where, is described in our [privacy policy](/legal/privacy).
- **Reports.** Anyone can report a page (see "How to report a page" below). We read every report.
- **A person.** An automated check or a report can hide a page while we look at it, but only a person removes a page, and only a person puts a hidden page back.

Names that look like a well-known brand next to words like "login" or "verify" can't be chosen as page names.

## What happens when a page breaks the rules

- **Hidden while we check it.** If the automated check flags a page, it is hidden until a person has looked at it. Visitors see a notice instead of the page.
- **Hidden.** We may hide a page while a report is open. A page reported as child sexual abuse material is hidden at once.
- **Removed.** If a page breaks these rules, we remove it. Its address stays blocked for a year, so nobody can publish under it and the links already shared don't lead to a new page.
- **Restored.** If we got it wrong, we put the page back as it was.

Every time we hide, remove or restore a page, we tell the person who published it what we did, which rule it falls under, whether an automated check was involved and whether a person reviewed it, and how to ask for a review. If you're signed in, we email you and show it on the page in your studio. If you published without an account, we email the address you gave for reminders, if any, and show it on the private link you got when you published.

**Downloads after a removal.** If you're signed in, you can still download a removed page from your studio for 30 days. After that, it's deleted for good. Pages hidden or removed under rule 4 can't be downloaded or previewed.

## Asking for a review

If you think we got it wrong, ask for a review: from the page in your studio, or from the private link you got when you published. Tell us in a few sentences why. A person reads every request and answers you by email and on the same page. You can also write to [abuse@kept.host](mailto:abuse@kept.host).

You can have one open request per page at a time, and up to three per page in 30 days. While a request is open, we don't delete the page.

## How to report a page

Use the form at [kept.host/report](/report), or email [abuse@kept.host](mailto:abuse@kept.host). Tell us the page's address, what's wrong and why. Your name and email are optional; if you give an email, we tell you when we receive the report and what we decided. Please only report in good faith.

## Other ways to get redress

Asking us for a review doesn't take away any of your other options. If you disagree with a decision we made about a page, or about a page you reported, you can also turn to an out-of-court dispute settlement body certified under Art. 21 of the EU Digital Services Act, or go to court.

## Ending use of kept

You can stop using kept at any time, and you can delete your account from your settings.

If someone breaks these rules seriously or again and again, we may remove their pages and close their account or block them from publishing. If someone keeps sending reports that are clearly unfounded, we may stop processing their reports for a while. In both cases we warn first, unless the breach is so serious that it can't wait, and we explain our decision.
