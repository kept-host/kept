## 2. The short version

- **No tracking:** no analytics, no ads, no tracking cookies. kept does not log page views.

- **Service providers:** we use a few to run kept (section 9). Some are US companies.

## 3. Visiting the website

**What your browser sends:** your IP address, the date and time, the page requested, and your browser and operating system.

**Who processes it:** our network and hosting providers, Cloudflare and Railway. They use it to deliver the site and protect it from attacks, and may keep it briefly in their logs. kept itself does not store logs of page views.

**Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in running a secure website.

## 4. Cookies

We only use cookies that are strictly necessary:

- **Cloudflare Turnstile**, our bot protection, may set a cookie when you use one of our forms (section 6).
- **A session cookie** keeps you signed in on app.kept.host. During pre-launch, only the operator can sign in.

These cookies need no consent under § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG. We use no analytics or advertising cookies.

## 5. Waitlist and founding-creator applications

**What we store:**
- your email address;
- which form you used;
- when you signed up and when you confirmed;
- for founding-creator applications only: the social profile link and the note you send us, and whether we accepted your application.

**Why:** to email you about kept (the launch, product news and offers). For founding applications, also to review your application and reply to you.

**Double opt-in:** we first send an email asking you to confirm. Nothing else is sent until you do. Sign-ups that are never confirmed are deleted after 30 days.

**Legal basis:** your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. For founding applications, also Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, meaning steps you asked us to take.

**Providing it is voluntary.** Without your email address we can't add you to the waitlist.

**Withdrawing consent:** every email has a one-click unsubscribe link, or you can email [[EMAIL]]. After you unsubscribe, we keep only your email address and the date, so that we never email you again (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

**How long we keep it:** until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete your data.

**Emails** are sent through Resend. We don't track whether you open our emails or click their links.

## 6. Bot protection (Cloudflare Turnstile)

**What it does:** our forms use Cloudflare Turnstile to tell people from bots.

**What it processes:** technical data from your browser, such as your IP address, browser characteristics and how you interact with the page.

**Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, protecting our forms from abuse.

Cloudflare's privacy policy: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/

## 7. Protection against abuse (rate limiting)

**Why:** to stop our forms and sign-in from being flooded, we count requests per visitor.

**What we store:** a scrambled form of your IP address (a hash with a key that changes every day), never the address itself. We delete these counts after 2 days.

**Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

## 8. Sign-in

During pre-launch, only the operator can sign in. If anyone else tries, we check the email address against that list and store nothing about them. We will describe sign-in here when it opens to everyone.

## 10. Your rights

**You can ask us for:**
- access to your data (Art. 15 GDPR);
- correction (Art. 16);
- deletion (Art. 17);
- restriction of processing (Art. 18);
- a copy in a portable format (Art. 20).

**Consent:** you can withdraw it at any time. That doesn't affect processing that happened before you withdrew (Art. 7(3)).

**Right to object:** where we rely on legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)), you can object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21).

To use any of these rights, email [[EMAIL]].

**Complaints:** you can complain to a data protection authority. The one responsible for us is the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen (LDI NRW): https://www.ldi.nrw.de

**No automated decisions:** we make no automated decisions about you and do no profiling.
